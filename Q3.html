The World Wide Web: Then vs Now

Original research concerning physice vs Current Uses

Original research concerning physice

Tim Berners Lee invented the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN. Purpose: To help scientists share research documents and access information. A research institute largely focused on studying the physics behind the tiniest particles.

Current Uses

People use the Web for education, shopping, and entertainment. For ex: watching videos (Youtube,...); Reading News; Virtual classes/meetings (Canvas, Zoom, etc.)

Comparison

Both the original Web and the Web today help people share and access information. Original purposes: Focused on researching (scientist) Current uses: Focused on Entertainment and everyday activities.
