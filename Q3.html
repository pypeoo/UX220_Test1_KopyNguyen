The World Wide Web: Then and Now

The Original Purpose

Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN, a particle physics laboratory. The Web helped scientists share research documents and access information from different computers.

Current Uses

People use the Web for education, shopping, entertainment and communication. Websites allow users to watch videos, read news and take online classes.

Similarities and Differences

Both the original Web and the Web today help people share and access information. The original purpose focused on scientific research, while today's Web serves a much wider audience and supports many everyday activities.
